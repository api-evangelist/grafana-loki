Over the past year, "agentic" has gone from an emerging buzzword to an integral part of worklife for many engineers—and that's just scratching the surface on all that's changed. With so much upheaval, there's never been a better time to capture the pulse of the industry, which is why we want you to take Grafana Labs' fifth annual Observability Survey . We're asking the questions you're probably already arguing about in Slack: Do you trust an agent to ship without review?
